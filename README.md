# 🎁 GiftAura Sales Analysis – Excel Data Analyst Project

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project provides an end-to-end exploratory data analysis and interactive dashboard solution built in **Microsoft Excel** for **GiftAura**, an e-commerce platform specializing in curated gifts for various occasions. 

By analyzing transactional customer order data, this project evaluates key revenue metrics, temporal purchasing patterns, high-performing product categories, top customer locations, and logistics performance. The resulting dashboard provides actionable insights to help business stakeholders optimize inventory planning, targeted marketing campaigns, and shipping operations.

### Findings and Conclusion

**🎯 Key Findings:**

*   **🎉 Festive Peaks Drive Bulk Sales:** Over 40% of annual revenue is generated during peak seasons in February and August.
*   **📦 Category Polarization:** A small number of product categories (Colors, Soft Toys, Sweets) generate the majority of transactions.
*   **⏰ Prime Purchasing Windows:** Customer purchases are concentrated in the evening (6 PM – 8 PM).
*   **📍 Emerging Regional Markets:** Order volume is high in growing urban centers like Imphal, Dhanbad, and Kavali, rather than major metropolitan areas.

**🚀 Strategic Business Recommendations:**

*   **📈 Capitalize on Peak Seasonal Spikes:** Increase digital marketing 2–3 weeks before February and August, and boost inventory for top sellers.
*   **🛍️ Revamp Underperforming Categories:** Bundle low-performing items (e.g., Plants, Mugs) with high-revenue products (e.g., Colors, Sweets) to increase basket size.
*   **⏰ Optimize Ad Scheduling:** Schedule promotional notifications, emails, and social media ads during peak customer activity (5 PM – 8 PM).
*   **🚚 Enhance Regional Supply Chain Operations:** Improve fulfillment logistics in high-volume regions (Imphal, Dhanbad, Kavali) to reduce delivery times from 5.53 to 3–4 days.
